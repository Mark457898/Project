Create and deliver an interactive sales presentation for a product or service of your choice. Your goal is to demonstrate that you can identify customer needs, communicate value, respond professionally, and persuade a potential customer.

A complete sales presentation designed around a specific product or service and a clearly defined customer.

• Explain important features and translate them into customer benefits.

• Anticipate possible customer concerns or objections.
• Develop professional responses to those concerns.
• End with an appropriate closing or call to action.

Sales, communication, persuasion, product research, audience analysis, listening, problem-solving, professionalism, and presentation skills.
